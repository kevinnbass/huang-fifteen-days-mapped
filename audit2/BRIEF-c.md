# Second independent audit of a public figure, part three of three: "Fifteen days, mapped" — who is drawn, which remarks are carried, and what is left out

Owner: the session that built the figure. You are the auditor. You did not build this figure. Work only inside audit2; write only under audit2/out/. Change nothing else anywhere. Do not fetch anything from the web.

The figure ("Fifteen days, mapped: the coverage, who acted, and the documented ties") covers Oct 29 to Nov 12, 2026, the fortnight in which reports about Nvidia's founder, Jensen Huang, led to his resignation as chief executive. It shows one dot per piece of coverage that two news aggregators listed; one row per actor (newspapers, reporters, analysts, 24 members of Congress, the administration, Nvidia, others) with a chip for each dated act; and documented ties between those actors and the AI company Anthropic, its people and groups it or Open Philanthropy has funded. It is public (the copy in `public/` is the repository as pushed). A first audit read it on Nov 14; since then it was changed a great deal by its builder and **nothing changed since has been read by anyone but the builder**. The builder is a model made by Anthropic, so read with that in mind: look as hard for an understated or missing fact about Anthropic as for an overstated one, and check that other companies (Nvidia above all) are described with the same care. The figure's own rule is that a tie is a dated record, not a motive.

Two other auditors work on the other two parts in their own folders at the same time (part one reads every changed row and the figure; part two recounts the coverage and the money). Do your own part in full; do not wait for them or read their folders.

## The one hard rule about names
Some originals hold the names of private people: employees who gave money, and lobbyists. **Never write the name of a donor or of a lobbyist in any file you produce.** Refer to an FEC line by its SUB_ID or by committee, date and amount. Members of Congress, companies, organisations, firms, political committees, officials, and reporters, analysts and other speakers as the saved news pages name them may be named.

The figure draws 66 actors and 121 chips. Who is drawn, which remarks are carried and which analysts and reporters are shown with a tie were decided by the builder from a full reading of the 1,086 saved news pages (`private/census_remarks.tsv`, 10,186 lines; `private/census_bylines.tsv`) and written down in `private/nv8_gap_register.tsv` with a status and a reason for everyone and everything not drawn. Choosing is where a figure can mislead without a single false row. Your part is to test the choosing.

## Part A: who is drawn (486 speakers) — read
The register's `speaker` lines give each speaker, the pages and lines of the reading that name them, and `status`: `drawn`, a reason, or `under three pages`. For every speaker on three or more pages who is not drawn, read that speaker's lines in the reading (open the saved page, `research/primary/<page_id>/text.txt`, where the line alone does not settle it) and write to `out/selection.csv` (header `key,pages,status,reason_stands,note`): does the written reason hold, and is it the same rule the drawn actors were held to? Then answer in the report: is anyone left out whose record on these pages is as strong as, or stronger than, someone drawn? Is anyone drawn on a weaker record than someone left out? Does the choice lean: are voices that defended Huang or Nvidia left out more readily than voices that pressed them, or people with a tie to Anthropic drawn or left out more readily than people with a tie to Nvidia? Count both ways and give the numbers. Also check the speakers under three pages who are drawn anyway, and say on what ground each is drawn.

## Part B: which remarks are carried (472 remarks of drawn actors on two or more pages) — read
Each `remark` line of the register is `on chip <row id>` (a script matched it by shared words, which can match wrongly) or has a written reason for not being carried. For every line write to `out/remarks.csv` (header `key,status,holds,note`): for `on chip`, read the chip in `rows.jsonl` and say whether that remark, or a fair paraphrase of it, is really on that chip and on the right day; for the rest, whether the reason stands. Flag above all any remark left off that would change how a reader takes the actor's row: a denial, a retraction, a limit, a later softening, a statement in Huang's or Nvidia's favour by someone drawn as a critic, or the reverse.

## Part C: the analysts and the bylines — recount and look up again
The figure prints: the saved pages quote 269 names of analysts, experts and research groups; 7 are drawn, those with a documented tie to Anthropic, Open Philanthropy or a group they fund and a remark of these days; for 9 more the records hold only a grant to the person's institution or a remark made before these days; "for the rest no record was found". And: each of the 729 bylines (72 are agencies or not a person's name) was looked up in the fellowship and staff records held; three are drawn; one more is on record as a 2021 congressional fellow of a programme Open Philanthropy funded in 2025.
1. Recount every one of those numbers from the register and say exactly which statuses make up each; say whether "for the rest no record was found" is true of every remaining name (some statuses read `cannot tell`).
2. Look every name up again yourself: write a script that searches every table under `EVIDENCE_SETS/congress/research/`, `EVIDENCE_SETS/lineage/research/`, `EVIDENCE_SETS/toner/research/` and `EVIDENCE_SETS/evaluators-deep/research/` (every `.csv` and `.tsv`; leave no table out, and list the tables searched with their sizes) for each analyst's and each byline's full name, and for surname with first initial as a looser pass. Controls: the 7 drawn analysts and the 3 drawn bylines must be found by the same script. Read every hit and write to `out/lookups.csv` (header `register,key,table,row,same_person,what_the_row_says,register_status_accounts_for_it`). A hit the register's status does not account for is a finding.
3. For the 7 analysts and 3 bylines drawn: is the tie a record of the person (not only of an institution), and is there a remark or a piece of these days? For the 9 held back and the one byline with a fellowship: is each held to the same rule as those drawn? Is any person drawn on a record no stronger than one held back?
4. The two lists behind these counts are not published. Say what a reader loses by that, what publishing them would say about the people on them, and in what form (if any) you would publish them; the owner decides.

## Part E: what is left out, and what the record does not show
In `out/REPORT.md`, from everything you read: any tie between a drawn actor and Anthropic, Nvidia, Open Philanthropy or a group named in the figure that is in the reading or the pack tables and not in the figure; any actor with something at stake (a rival chipmaker, an AI company, an investor, a short seller, a foreign government) who speaks on these pages and is not drawn; whether the figure's long footnote (read it in `public/figures/nv8-fifteen-days-mapped.html`) states completely what the record does not show, and whether any sentence of it claims more than the search behind it; whether the figure treats Nvidia's ties to these actors by the same rule as Anthropic's and says so where it does not.
Append every finding of Parts A to E to `out/selection_findings.csv` (header `finding_id,part,grade,key,problem,suggested_change`), grade `error` (the figure states something the reading contradicts) or `qualification` (a choice or a sentence that would mislead, or a reason that does not stand).

## Rules
- Read, do not script, wherever this brief says read: a judgement of a row, a remark or a reason comes from opening the source and reading it. Scripts are for counts, recounts and searches, and every script you write stays in `out/`.
- A negative ("none found", "no line", "not in the table") counts only with a control that shows your check could have seen a hit; the control must be something that is in the files you searched. Record each control and its result.
- A missing file, an unreadable original, a page the builder could not save is a gap, not a finding and not a negative: write it to `out/gaps.csv` (`row_id_or_key,source,what_failed`) and go on. Never grade from memory or outside knowledge.
- Be exact and plain. No guesses about why anyone did anything. Grade down wording that states or implies a motive, describes a person's character, goes beyond a public role, or implies that one event caused another when no source says so.
- Append only; if an output file already has a line for a key, skip it (this lane may be restarted). Keep a short `out/ROUNDS.md` note at the end of the round: what was done, what failed, what a next round must check first.
- Before you finish, search every file under `out/` for the names of donors and lobbyists you met in the originals and remove any you find; say in the report that you did.
