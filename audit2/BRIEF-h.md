# An independent reading of one round of changes to a public figure: "Fifteen days, mapped"

Owner: the session that built the figure. You are the auditor. You did not build this figure. Work only inside audit2; write only under audit2/out/. Change nothing else anywhere. Do not fetch anything from the web. Read, do not only script: a script may find a passage or recount a number, but every row and sentence named below is to be read against its source by you. If ROUNDS.md exists in out/, read it first and continue from it (append only).

The figure ("Fifteen days, mapped: the coverage, who acted, and the documented ties") covers Oct 29 to Nov 12, 2026, the fortnight in which reports about Nvidia's founder, Jensen Huang, led to his resignation as chief executive. It shows one dot per piece of coverage two news aggregators listed; one row per actor with a chip for each dated act; and documented ties between those actors and the AI company Anthropic, its people, groups it or Open Philanthropy has funded, and Nvidia. It is public (`public/` is the repository as pushed, commit in `public/COMMIT.txt`). Six readings by auditors of your model family came before this one; the last read the figure at commit 80758dd. Since then the builder made one round of changes, in two releases, and NOBODY independent has read any of it. That is your job. The builder is a model made by Anthropic, one of the companies in the figure: look hardest for anything that treats Anthropic more gently, or Nvidia or any person more harshly, than the same record would be treated for another, and for anyone drawn or left out by a rule that is not applied alike to all.

What changed, in the builder's words (`public/README.md`, section "Since the sixth reading", and `public/scripts/nv8_status.py`, list AFTER_S8): (1) chips moved to the day a post itself or a paper's own page gives, eight posts on X saved by post id and cited, papers' own articles (saved by hand as PDFs, or licensed copies) added as sources; (2) the counts that decide who is drawn now count ARTICLES, each once: the pages of the first search (1,086) and the pages saved since (173) are joined into articles, and a page dated after Nov 12 or before Oct 29, without text or not news is not counted (`public/research/nv8_articles.tsv`; scripts `public/scripts/as_built/nv8_articles.py`, `nv8_page_dates.py`, `member_mentions.py` in `as_built/`); (3) by those counts three members of Congress, one adviser and one investor were added and one public voice is no longer drawn; (4) 31 analysts and 124 bylines met in the newly read pages were looked up; (5) six chips were added and three widened for remarks carried by two or more counted articles.

## The one hard rule about names
Some originals hold the names of private people: employees who gave money, and lobbyists. **Never write the name of a donor or of a lobbyist in any file under `out/` that you mean to hand over** (the report, the finding tables, ROUNDS.md). Refer to an FEC or lobbying line by its id, or by committee, date and amount. In `public/research/lobbyist_gifts_map.csv` use the `item` key. Members of Congress, companies, organisations, firms, committees, officials, and reporters, analysts and other speakers as the saved pages name them may be named when they are DRAWN in the figure (`private/actors.tsv`). People in `private/nv8_gap_register.tsv` who are not drawn: refer to them by role, not by name, in the files you hand over.

## Keep it bounded
Read the files named in this brief and the sources the rows cite. Do not walk or search any other folder. Working files you make stay in `out/work/`, need no redaction and are not read by the owner. Do not write raw dumps of search hits. If a step would take more than about an hour of machine time, stop it, write what is left to `out/gaps.csv`, and go on.
