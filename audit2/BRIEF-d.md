Owner: the session that built the figure. You are the auditor. You did not build this figure. Work only inside audit2; write only under audit2/out/. Change nothing else anywhere. Do not fetch anything from the web.

The figure ("Fifteen days, mapped: the coverage, who acted, and the documented ties") covers Oct 29 to Nov 12, 2026, the fortnight in which reports about Nvidia's founder, Jensen Huang, led to his resignation as chief executive. It shows one dot per piece of coverage two news aggregators listed; one row per actor with a chip for each dated act; and documented ties between those actors and the AI company Anthropic, its people, groups it or Open Philanthropy has funded, and Nvidia. It is public (the copy in `public/` is the repository as pushed). Three auditors of your model family read it on Nov 15 and raised 11 errors and 145 qualifications. The builder then changed the figure in answer, and **nobody but the builder has read those changes**. That is your job. The builder is a model made by Anthropic, so read with that in mind: look as hard for an understated or missing fact about Anthropic as for an overstated one, and check that Nvidia and everyone else is described with the same care. The figure's own rule is that a tie is a dated record, not a motive.

## Rules
- Read, do not script, wherever this brief says read: a judgement of a row, a remark or a reason comes from opening the source and reading it. Scripts are for counts, recounts and the one named search; every script stays in `out/`.
- A negative counts only with a control that shows your check could have seen a hit; the control must be something that is in the files you searched. Record each control and its result.
- A missing file or an unreadable original is a gap, not a finding and not a negative: write it to `out/gaps.csv` (`row_id_or_key,source,what_failed`) and go on. Never grade from memory or outside knowledge.
- Be exact and plain. No guesses about why anyone did anything. Grade down wording that states or implies a motive, describes a person's character, goes beyond a public role, or implies that one event caused another when no source says so.
- Append only; if an output file already has a line for a key, skip it (this lane may be restarted). End the round with a short `out/ROUNDS.md` note: what was done, what failed, what a next round must check first.
