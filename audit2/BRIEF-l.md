## The one hard rule about names
Some originals hold the names of private people: employees who gave money, and lobbyists. **Never write the name of a donor or of a lobbyist in any file under `out/` that you mean to hand over** (the report, the finding tables, ROUNDS.md). A campaign committee that carries a lobbyist's name names that lobbyist: do not write it either. Refer to an FEC or lobbying line by its id, or by committee, date and amount; in the lobbyists' table use the `item` key. Members of Congress, companies, organisations, firms, political committees that do not carry a lobbyist's name, and officials may be named. Reporters, analysts and other speakers may be named only when they are DRAWN in the figure (`private/actors.tsv`); anyone else, refer to by role.
