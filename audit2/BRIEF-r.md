## The figure's rules you are checking the rows against
- A chip is one dated act, worded from a saved report. Words inside double quotation marks must be in the source as quoted (an ellipsis or square brackets mark a cut or an insertion).
- An act sits on the day its source gives: a day the source states, or one its context fixes (a remark dated with others like it; an answer to something that happened that day in a report of that day). Failing that it sits on the day the source was first published, and then the chip has a dashed left edge ("the act itself may be earlier"). Where a cited page was changed after it was first published, a passage taken from it may be newer than the chip's day, and the note says for how many. In `rows.jsonl` and `public/research/nv8_rows.tsv` such a row has `reported_by_others` true.
- Two acts of one actor on one day share a chip; where only one of the two is dated by its report, the edge is solid and the words say which is the report.
- Where a chip rests on one outlet's account of another's report, it says so. Each reported exchange carries the denial its own source reports.
- No motive, no characterisation of people; public roles only.
- Refusals (the rule the sixteenth reading proposed, now the figure's): every explicit refusal by a drawn actor to comment on its own conduct or part in the matter is drawn, once for each documented response, with any answer the same source prints beside it; refusals of one day to several outlets share one act that names each; a report that no reply had come to a request is not a refusal and is drawn for no one; a department's statements and refusals are drawn on its secretary's row.
- An act is drawn when a saved report gives it, it is on the matter and no chip of that actor already carries the same occurrence: a similar remark on another occasion is its own act; a later article repeating a dated remark is not. Identity of occurrence needs proof (the same post, the same document, the same occasion), not a similar view.
- When the builder read the quoted remarks that an earlier judgment had set aside as part of an occasion already drawn, it added a part of a drawn statement as an act of the same day when it was one of four things: an answer about the actor's own conduct, a hedge or a denial, the main message of a statement whose chip quoted a lesser phrase, or a concrete act. A reason given for a position already drawn was not added.
