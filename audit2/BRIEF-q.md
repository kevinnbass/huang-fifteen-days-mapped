## The figure's rules you are checking the rows against
- A chip is one dated act, worded from a saved report. Words inside double quotation marks must be in the source as quoted (an ellipsis or square brackets mark a cut or an insertion).
- An act sits on the day its source gives: a day the source states, or one its context fixes (a remark dated with others like it; an answer to something that happened that day in a report of that day). Failing that it sits on the day the source was first published, and then the chip has a dashed left edge ("the act itself may be earlier"). Where the page was changed after that day the passage may be newer than the chip's day, and the note says for how many. In `rows.jsonl` and `public/research/nv8_rows.tsv` such a row has `reported_by_others` true.
- Two acts of one actor on one day share a chip; where only one of the two is dated by its report, the edge is solid and the words say which is the report.
- Where a chip rests on one outlet's account of another's report, it says so. Each reported exchange carries the denial its own source reports.
- No motive, no characterisation of people; public roles only.
- A refusal to comment on the actor's own part is drawn when two or more counted articles carry it for one report, or when it is the actor's only recorded answer on the matter; a line that no reply had come to a request for comment is not drawn for anyone.
- An act is drawn when a saved report gives it, it is on the matter and no chip of that actor already carries the same occurrence: a similar remark on another occasion is its own act; a later article repeating a dated remark is not.
